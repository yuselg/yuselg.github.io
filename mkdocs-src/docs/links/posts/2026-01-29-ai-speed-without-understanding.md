**Anthropic Report, 29 January 2026**: When humans subcontract their thinking to machines, their intellect is eroded precisely at the moment when reason and judgment are needed to detect fabrications and hallucinations endemic to large language models. Active reasoning-forming and testing hypotheses, tracing causes, interrogating outputs—is essential to control the significant risks inherent in these tools.
