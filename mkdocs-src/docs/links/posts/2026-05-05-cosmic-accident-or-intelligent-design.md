Are we a cosmic accident? While the public school system still teaches that we are here by undirected processes that did not have us in mind, the evidence across biology, chemistry, and physics increasingly points in a different direction. From the digital code in the DNA of a cell to the fine-tuning of the universe, modern science increasingly reveals a world of plan and purpose.  [Lag B'Omer, 5786]

For more on the fine tuning of nature for carbon based life, view Michel Denton: [Fitness of Nature for Carbon based Life](https://youtu.be/znGyrGWa004?si=DBOSZqDT9lr_DDwj):

> 1. Evidence that the laws of nature are uniquely fine tuned (fit) for the complex chemistry of life on earth. 2. Evidence that nature is uniquely fit for advanced terrestrial biological beings close to the physiology of modern humans. 3. Evidence that our species is uniquely fit to acquire a profound knowledge and understanding of the natural world.

The Chovos Halevovos (Duties of the Mind) expresses best what we should conclude from “signs of wisdom” (סימני חכמה) we see in the cosmos: 

"There are men who say that the world came has come into existence by chance, without a Creator Who caused it and formed it. I find this rather astonishing!  How could any rational human being, in his right mind, entertain such a notion? … Surely you would agree that, if a man were accidentally to spill some ink onto a blank sheet of paper, it is impossible that it would take the shape of orderly writing and legible lines, as are written with a pen. If a person were to show us [a page of] orderly writing - that could only have been written with a pen - and say that ink had spilled out onto the page and had, on its own, taken the shape of written characters, we would be quick to deny it to his face, as this could not have come about without a purposeful designer. Now, since this appears to us to be an impossibility in regard to written characters, [which are merely] products of convention, how can one say - in regard to something more subtle in its art, more difficult in its achievement and of boundless depth - that it is without the intent of a Designer, without the wisdom of an omnipotent Intelligence?"
